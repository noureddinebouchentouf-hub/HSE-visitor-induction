Mandatory Basic PPE

Wearing these Personal Protective Equipment items is required at all times for access to the production areas of the Oggaz plant.

Safety Helmet

Obligatory

Protects the head against falling objects, collisions with structures, and projections.

Safety Shoes

Obligatory

Equipped with reinforced toes to protect against heavy falling loads and puncture-resistant soles.

High-Visibility Vest

Obligatory

Enables you to be immediately spotted by machine operators and other site workers.

Safety Glasses

Obligatory

Protect eyes against flying particles, dust, and accidental projections of materials.

New

Hearing Protection (Stop Bruit)

Noisy Zones

Ear defenders or earplugs. Mandatory in mills, compressor rooms, and kiln area.

New

Dust Mask

Dusty Zones

FFP2 mask required to protect against inhalation of clinker, gypsum, and cement dust.
